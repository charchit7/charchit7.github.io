Research

I work on image generation, virtual try-on, and the evaluation of generative models.

Currently thinking about

  • Try-on that keeps you, you

    Turning catalog-style photos into natural, personal ones without losing the person in them.

  • What did the model actually learn?

    Exploring representation learning and explainability, including data attribution: tracing a generated image back to the training data that shaped it.

  • Did the model get better?

    Evaluating generative models and the data behind them more carefully than a single headline number.
